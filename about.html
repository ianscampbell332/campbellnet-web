Our Story

25 Years. One Region. Real Expertise.

CampbellNet Solutions has been building and maintaining network infrastructure across upstate New York for a quarter century.

Not a Vendor. A Partner.

CampbellNet Solutions was founded by Don Campbell, a network engineer who's been building and maintaining infrastructure across upstate New York since before WiFi existed. The company has grown steadily by doing one thing well: showing up, doing the work, and being easy to reach when something needs attention.

We're not a national MSP that treats Buffalo like a market segment. We're a regional team that knows the school districts, the municipalities, the hospitals, and the businesses we serve — often by name. When you call us, you get someone who knows your network.

Our focus is the upstate New York corridor — Buffalo east to Albany, north through the Adirondacks, and everywhere in between. If your organization is in this region, we're your neighbors.
